## RLCraft Death Overhaul — coming soon

Dying in RLCraft costs you everything. Turning on `keepInventory` makes it cost nothing. This is the setting in between.

**When you die:**
- You keep your gear — armour, hotbar, both hands, baubles, and your backpack
- You drop your main inventory
- **Your dropped items never despawn.** No 5 minute timer. Your death pile waits for you
- Everything you kept takes 10% durability
- You lose a heart of maximum health, permanently

**The minimum is the part I like.** It sits at 10 hearts — exactly what you start with. So the hearts you were born with can never be taken, only the ones you earned from heart containers.

Dying while you're still learning the pack costs you nothing. But every heart container you spend puts itself on the line, so using one stops being free and starts being a decision.

Configurable throughout: the heart cost, a grace period so you can set N deaths before anything is charged, which slots survive, and exemptions by dimension or damage type.

1.12.2 / Forge. Requires Scaling Health.
